1. Summary

The MVP set out to let customers move through one simple journey online, and to reduce the store's reliance on manual WhatsApp ordering:

Discover→ Understand→ Choose→ Buy→ Receive→ Reorder

We checked each agreed item against the application. The tiles below count the 98 items in section 2.

Where the project stands The agreed functionality is built and tested. Customers can browse the Square catalogue, search, shop by country, read stories linked to products and recipes, cook from recipes, pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Interac e-Transfer, collect in store, earn Square Loyalty points, reorder in one tap, ask to be emailed when an item is back in stock, and report a problem online. Staff run orders (with a full timeline), customer cases, content, analytics and settings from the Operations Portal. The automated test suite (294 checks) passes in full.
The immediate next step: going live On receipt of the final project payment, the production environment will be provisioned, the website deployed and the platform made accessible to customers. The accessibility audit and Core Web Vitals measurement are then carried out on the live website.

Outside the current scope

  • Delivery / logistics provider. No driver allocation, last-mile tracking, provider statuses or delivery analytics. Checkout already has a delivery option with a postal-code check and fee, and managers can switch delivery on or off. It is currently set up for Pickup.
  • Ask Asha AI. The floating Asha button is hidden on the homepage and the Meet Asha page says "Coming soon".
  • Community Hub. A teaser page is live at /community with an email sign-up for launch news. Events, ticketing, memberships and profiles remain Phase 2.
  • Coupons and promotional codes, WhatsApp integration, SMS integration, and retention-event analytics. Loyalty figures are available on the staff Loyalty screen.

4. Recommended Next Steps

In order. Item 1 is what moves the platform from finished to live.

1Go live

On receipt of the final project payment, the production server will be provisioned, the website deployed and the platform made accessible to customers. This includes switching Square to the live account and keys, registering the live webhook, and moving hosting to paid plans so background tasks run on schedule (Square sync, reminders, back-in-stock emails and Brevo retries).

2Analytics & conversion tracking

Event tracking and the Analytics & Measurement module are in place. Add the Google Analytics 4 measurement ID at go-live to send the same discovery, commerce and pickup events to Google Analytics.

3Brevo marketing email

Add the Brevo API key and newsletter list ID at go-live. Existing and new subscribers then flow into Brevo, ready for newsletter, new-arrivals and promotions campaigns.

4Product commissioning

Complete product descriptions in Square and the Operations Portal (ingredients, preparation/use, dietary and storage information), and link stories to their products and recipes.

5Richer product information (optional)

If you later want ingredients, preparation, dietary and storage information in separate fields, these can be added. Today they are entered in the product description.

6After launch: accessibility audit & Core Web Vitals

Carry out the formal WCAG accessibility audit and measure Core Web Vitals on the live website.
