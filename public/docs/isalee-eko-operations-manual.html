Section 1: Access & Authentication

1.1 Accessing the Operations Portal

The store operations portal is completely isolated from the public storefront to maintain operational privacy and protect customer payment records.

  • Login URL: https://isaleeeko.ca/admin/login (or http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin/login in local network)
  • Required Credentials: Your registered staff email address and password.
1 Enter Your Credentials

Type your assigned staff email address and password into the respective fields. If you are using a shared store tablet, do not check automatic browser password saves.

2 Click "Sign In to Operations"

Upon verification, the system will direct you directly into the Operations Command Center.

1.2 Two-Tier Staff Role Separation

Ìsàlèè Èkó employs a strict two-tier security model to prevent accidental modification of financial keys while allowing store staff full speed on daily fulfillment:

Capability / Screen Store Associate Associate Store Manager Manager
Operations Dashboard Full View (Orders, Sales, Low Stock) Full View (Orders, Sales, Low Stock)
Orders Queue & Packing Fulfill, Pack, Complete Orders Fulfill, Pack, Refund, Complete
Products & Stock Quantities Edit Stock Counts & Variations Full Catalog & Price Editing
Categories & Recipes Create, Edit, Upload Photos Full Hierarchy & Sync Control
Store Hours & Holiday Closures View Operating Times Edit Hours & Emergency Closure
Square Tokens & Payment Keys Hidden (No Access) Full Access
Staff Roster & Passwords Hidden (No Access) Add, Reset, Deactivate

1.3 Changing Your Password (Self-Service)

Any staff member can change their password at any time without asking a manager:

  1. Look at the top-right corner of the topbar next to your name initials.
  2. Click the Key icon (Change My Password).
  3. In the drawer that appears, type your Current Password, your New Password (minimum 6 characters), and confirm it.
  4. Click Update Password. Your new credentials take effect immediately.
Deactivated Staff Security Guard If an employee leaves the company and a manager marks them as Deactivated, their active session is terminated instantly. Even if they type the correct password, the portal will reject access and display an account suspension notice.

Section 2: Operations Command Center

The Dashboard Overview (/admin) serves as the morning launchpad and live nerve center for the retail grocery store.

2.1 Live KPI Metric Tiles

Scorecard Tile What It Measures Action Required
Today's Gross Sales Total revenue from orders placed today in Moncton time (AST). Review daily sales velocity against store targets.
Orders Placed Today Count of new orders received since midnight. Helps pace kitchen and packaging staffing.
Active Fulfillment Orders marked Placed or Paid awaiting completion. Must be cleared to 0 before the end of the shift.
Low Stock Alerts Number of product items with 5 or fewer units left in inventory. Alert warehouse suppliers or restock retail shelves.

2.2 Real-Time Store Operating Pill

At the top of the dashboard, a colored badge displays the exact public status of the physical store:

  • ● Open until 7:00 PM — Store is within regular scheduled hours and accepting shoppers.
  • ● Opens at 10:00 AM — Store is currently closed overnight; will open automatically at the scheduled time.
  • ● Closed: Holiday / Emergency — Scheduled closure or emergency toggle is active.

2.3 Urgent Orders Queue (First-In, First-Out)

The dashboard displays the 5 oldest pending orders that need packing right away. Clicking on any order number takes staff directly into the packing desk.

2.4 Low Stock & Depleted Inventory Panel

Displays products where stock is dangerously low (≤ 5 units). If a customer purchases the last item on the storefront, the variation automatically flips to "Sold Out" online to prevent overselling.

Section 3: Orders Queue & Fulfillment Workflow

Accessible from the sidebar under Fulfillment & Operations → Orders Queue (/admin/orders).

3.1 Order Lifecycle Stages

Status Badge Meaning Staff Responsibility
Placed Order submitted by customer; payment authorization initiated. Review order items and begin pulling groceries from shelves.
Paid / Packing Payment confirmed via Square. Items are currently being packed. Bag items, verify batch quantities, and print packing slip.
Ready for Pickup Customer selected in-store pickup; parcel placed in pickup shelving. Customer receives automatic pickup notification. Hand over on arrival.
Out for Delivery Order handed to local Moncton delivery driver. Package en route to customer's residential address.
Completed Goods received by customer; transaction finalized. No further action required. Order archived in history.

3.2 Step-by-Step Order Packing Guide

1 Locate Order on Queue

Filter by Active Orders or search by customer name, phone number, or 5-digit Order Number (e.g. #ORD-8291).

2 Open Order Detail Drawer

Click on the order row. Inspect all requested variations (e.g., "Yam Tubers (Medium 3-Pack)" or "Honey Beans 5kg") and check any customer delivery instructions.

3 Pick & Pack Goods

Collect items from store shelves or freezer. Verify perishable items (meat, frozen fish, plantain) are properly temperature-insulated.

4 Update Fulfillment Status

Change status to Ready for Pickup or Completed. The system records the timestamp and staff audit trail.

Split Fulfillment Types Orders are clearly tagged as either Local Moncton Delivery (with street address and postal code) or In-Store Express Pickup. Keep pickup bags in the designated pickup counter behind the till.

Section 4: Products & Inventory Management

Accessible from the sidebar under Catalog & Content → Products & Inventory (/admin/products).

4.1 Catalog Overview

The products screen lists all grocery items available in the store, organized with their respective brand, country of origin (Nigeria, Ghana, Jamaica, Canada), and price variations.

4.2 Managing Product Variations & Stock Counts

African grocery items often sell in multiple formats (e.g., Single Tuber vs 3-Pack; 2kg bag vs 10kg sack). Each format has its own independent stock level.

1 Adjusting Stock Quantities

Click Edit on any product. Scroll down to the Variations & Pricing table. Directly update the numerical Stock Quantity field (e.g., received 25 new bags of Golden Penny Semovita). Click Save Changes.

2 Setting Featured Status

Toggle the Featured on Storefront checkbox to spotlight seasonal items (like fresh yellow garri or fresh scotch bonnets) directly on the homepage banner.

Square Inventory Synchronization If an in-store customer purchases an item through the physical Square POS register, the web catalog stock reduces in real time to prevent duplicate orders.

Section 5: Categories & Sub-Categories Hierarchy

Accessible from the sidebar under Catalog & Content → Categories & Sub-Cats (/admin/categories).

5.1 Catalog Hierarchy Model

Categories follow a two-tier organizational hierarchy:

  • Parent Departments (Root): Broad supermarket departments (e.g., "Flour, Grains & Rice", "Spices & Seasonings", "Snacks & Drinks", "Frozen Meats & Seafood").
  • Sub-Categories: Specialized groupings nested beneath parents (e.g., "Fufu Flour", "Poundo Yam", "Suya Pepper").

5.2 Adding a New Category

1 Click "+ New Category"

Type the customer-facing name (e.g., "Dried Fish & Stockfish").

2 Select Parent Department

If this is a sub-category, pick its parent department from the dropdown. If creating a brand-new top-level department, leave it set to -- None (Top-Level Department) --.

3 Choose Visual Icon Badge

Click on any of the pre-configured high-definition icon badges in the visual palette, or type an emoji directly into the icon box.

4 Select or Upload Category Photo

Staff can pick from the pre-loaded Store Photo Library with 1 click, or switch to Upload from Device to drag and drop a clean JPG/PNG image from a camera or phone.

Section 6: Kitchen Recipes & Culinary Content

Accessible from the sidebar under Catalog & Content → Kitchen Recipes (/admin/recipes).

6.1 Why Recipes Drive Grocery Basket Size

Ìsàlèè Èkó recipes are not just cooking instructions — they are an interactive sales driver. When customers view a recipe (like "Authentic Nigerian Egusi Soup"), the storefront provides a single "Add All Ingredients to Cart" button that adds the exact ground egusi, palm oil, crayfish, and stockfish directly into their basket.

6.2 Publishing a Recipe

  1. Click + New Recipe in the top-right corner.
  2. Fill in:
    • Recipe Title: e.g., "Smoky Jollof Rice (Party Style)".
    • Origin / Country: Nigeria, Ghana, etc.
    • Difficulty & Times: Prep time (minutes), Cook time (minutes), and Servings count.
  3. Link Store Ingredients: For each required ingredient, link it to the actual product item in your store inventory so shoppers can purchase it with one tap.
  4. Cooking Steps: Type simple, numbered step-by-step instructions.
  5. Click Publish Recipe. It appears on the public storefront immediately.

Section 8: Square POS & Payment Gateways

Accessible to Store Managers under Store Controls → Payment Methods (/admin/payments).

8.1 Payment Channels Supported

  • Square Web Payments: Secure tokenized checkout for Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Interac Debit.
  • Cash on Express In-Store Pickup: Allows shoppers to pay at the physical counter when collecting their order.
  • Interac e-Transfer: Manual e-transfer payment instructions for local shoppers.

8.2 Safe Handling of Square Credentials

The store's Square connection is governed by three secure keys:

  1. Square Application ID: Identifies your registered Square merchant application.
  2. Square Access Token: The authorization key that allows the store to charge cards and sync inventory.
  3. Square Location ID: Identifies your physical retail store register in Moncton.
Security Notice for Store Owners Never share your Square Access Token via unencrypted email or text message. Only Store Managers have access to the Payment Methods screen. Store Associates are blocked from viewing these credentials.

Section 9: Staff & Access Management (Handover System)

Accessible to Store Managers under Store Controls → Staff & Access (/admin/staff).

This dedicated portal was engineered specifically for the client handover so the store owner can independently add new employees, issue passwords, and manage departing staff without calling an IT developer.

9.1 Onboarding a New Employee

1 Click "+ Add Staff Member"

In the top-right corner of the Staff Management portal, click the red button.

2 Enter Employee Details
  • Full Name: e.g., "Bukola Adeleke".
  • Email Address: This will be their unique username to log in (e.g., bukola@isaleeeko.ca).
  • Phone Number: Optional contact number.
3 Select Staff Role
  • Store Associate — For cashier, packaging, and kitchen staff. Can fulfill orders and update stock. Payment tokens and staff controls are hidden.
  • Store Manager — For owners and head managers. Full administrative authority.
4 Copy Temporary Password

The system auto-generates a secure temporary password (e.g. temp8391!#). Click the Copy button and send it to the employee via WhatsApp, SMS, or email.

5 Employee Logs In & Updates Password

The employee goes to /admin/login, enters their email and temporary password, and uses the key icon in the topbar to change it to their personal password.

9.2 Resetting an Employee's Forgotten Password

  1. Find the employee in the Staff Roster Table.
  2. Click Edit.
  3. In the yellow "Reset Password" box, click Generate Random (or type a new custom password).
  4. Click Save Changes. Provide the new password to the employee.

9.3 Handling Departed Staff (Deactivation vs Deletion)

  • Instant Deactivation: Click Deactivate. The employee is immediately locked out from the portal. They cannot log in, but their past fulfillment audit logs remain intact.
  • Revoke Access (Delete): Click the Trash icon. If the employee has processed orders, the system automatically revokes all admin rights while preserving order audit history. If no orders exist, the account is cleanly erased.
  • Self-Protection Guard: A manager cannot deactivate or delete their own active account, preventing accidental lockouts.

Section 10: Daily Store Checklists & Emergency Recovery

10.1 Morning Opening Checklist (15 Minutes Before Opening)

Step Action Location in Admin
1 Sign in to operations portal on the store tablet/counter computer. /admin/login
2 Check Store Status Pill to confirm hours are scheduled for today. Dashboard Header
3 Review Active Fulfillment Backlog for overnight web orders. /admin/orders
4 Check Low Stock Alerts for items that sold out overnight. /admin/products
5 Print packing slips for morning pickup slots. Orders Desk

10.2 Evening Closing Checklist (At Store Closing)

Step Action Location in Admin
1 Verify all orders marked "Ready for Pickup" were collected by customers. /admin/orders
2 Record any delivered local delivery batches as "Completed". /admin/orders
3 Review Today's Gross Sales tile for end-of-day register reconciliation. Dashboard Overview
4 Sign out of the operations portal (click door/exit icon in topbar). Topbar Profile

10.3 Emergency Terminal Recovery (For IT & Developers)

If store managers ever lose all passwords or accidentally lock themselves out, access can be instantly restored in 5 seconds directly from the server terminal without database surgery:

Open a terminal on the host server and run:

php artisan staff:create owner@isaleeeko.ca --name="Store Owner" --password="EmergencyPassword123!" --role=manager

This command creates or reactivates the account as a verified Store Manager with full access restored immediately.
