1.3 Changing Your Password (Self-Service)

  1. At the top right of the portal, next to your name, click the Key icon (Change My Password).
  2. Enter your Current Password, then your New Password (at least 6 characters) twice.

Click the door / exit icon next to your name. Always sign out on shared devices.

3.1 Finding Orders

  • Tabs: Active Queue · New · Preparing · Ready · Uncollected · e-Transfers to Confirm · Completed · Cancelled · All History.
  • Search: order number, customer name, phone or email.
  • Filters: In-Store Pickup / Local Delivery, and Paid Online / Unpaid (e-Transfer).

3.3 Step-by-Step Packing Guide

1Open the Order

Click Details (or the order row) to open the order drawer: items and sizes, customer contact, pickup slot or delivery address, payment status and total.

2Accept & Start Preparing

Press Accept & Start Preparing. There is no separate "Accepted" step: this tells the team and the customer that the order is accepted and being prepared.

3Pick & Pack

Collect items from shelves and freezer. Keep frozen and fresh items (meat, fish, plantain) insulated.

4Mark Ready, then Hand Over

Press Mark Ready for Collection and put the bag on the pickup shelf behind the till. When the customer arrives, press Complete & Hand Over.

3.4 Item Unavailable? No Silent Substitutions

Never swap an item without the customer's agreement. In the order drawer, next to the item:

  1. Click Item Unavailable, choose a replacement option (or Full Refund), add an optional note, and press Initiate Substitution (Call Customer).
  2. Call the customer and record whether you reached them. Then record Customer Approved or Customer Declined (Refund).
  3. If you can't reach them, use Resend to email the question. The customer can answer from the link.
  4. If the item turns up on the shelf, choose Cancel request (item found on shelf).
Response Window Questions not answered within the window set in Store Settings (default 15 minutes) are refunded automatically for that item. The same happens if you press Mark Ready or Complete while a question is still open.

3.5 Interac e-Transfer Orders

The e-Transfers to Confirm tab lists orders waiting for money. When the e-Transfer arrives in the store's bank account, open the order and press Confirm e-Transfer Received. The customer is emailed and the order joins the queue. If the money never arrives, press Cancel (Not Paid). Nothing was charged, so there is nothing to refund.

3.6 Uncollected Orders & Rescheduling

  • Customers get a reminder if they miss their pickup time. After the grace period (Store Settings), the order is held for staff or cancelled automatically, depending on your settings.
  • Mark as Uncollected moves a pickup order to the Uncollected tab and emails the customer.
  • Reschedule Pickup lets you choose a new day and time slot.
  • Cancel & Policy Refund asks for a reason and a refund level (100%, 50% or 0%). The refund goes back through Square.

3.7 Order Timeline

At the bottom of every order drawer, the Order Timeline lists each status change: for example Paid · new order, Accepted · preparing, Ready · awaiting pickup, Picked up · completed. Each one shows the date and time in Atlantic Time and the staff member who made it. Use it to answer "when was this ready?" questions from customers.

Stuck Card Payments If a card payment timed out, opening the Orders Queue checks Square automatically and settles it (at most every 5 minutes). Customers are never charged twice.

Section 4: Issues Desk

Sidebar: Fulfillment & Operations → Issues Desk (/admin/issues). Use it for any complaint about a completed order. The red badge counts open cases.

4.1 Case Types

  • Damaged / Missing / Quality Issues: problems with food or groceries.
  • Non-Food Returns (Change of Mind): non-food items within the return window.
  • Food Safety & Allergen Alerts: treat as urgent. They have their own tile.
  • General Enquiries (Website): questions sent through the website Help form, sometimes without an order.

4.2 Logging a Case Taken by Phone, WhatsApp, Email or In Store

1Log Customer Complaint

Enter the order number and press Locate Order. Choose the channel (phone, WhatsApp, email, in store) and the case type.

2Select Items & Describe

Tick the affected items and quantities, describe the problem, and press Open Case.

4.3 Working a Case

Click Inspect Case to see the order, items, photos and the Case Diary & Timeline. Use Record Note for internal notes. Then choose a resolution:

ResolutionWhat Happens
Execute Policy RefundRefunds the affected items through Square (optionally including the delivery fee) and emails the customer.
Schedule Replacement OrderCreates a replacement for the affected items.
Send Resolution Options to CustomerLets the customer choose a refund or a replacement. The case shows Awaiting Choice.
Decline Customer ClaimCloses the case with a standard reason (e.g. food change of mind) or your own note.

Statuses: New Intake → Reviewing → Awaiting Customer → Resolved / Declined. Cases that pass the staff response target (default 24 hours) without a first reply are flagged Breached. Check the Issues Desk at least twice a day.

Report windows (e.g. 24 hours for food) and return windows are set in Store Settings → Returns & Issues Policy.

4.4 Cases from the Website (Report a Problem)

Customers can open a case themselves on the Help & Contact page (/help), linked from the header Help menu, the footer and every order page. These cases show the channel website and the contact details the customer typed.

  • With order items: if the customer entered their order number and email and ticked the affected items, the case works like any other. Refund, replace, offer choices or decline.
  • Questions or no items: the drawer shows Reply by Email and Mark Resolved. Reply to the customer, then mark the case resolved. If the reply leads to a refund, use Log Customer Complaint with the order number and tick the items.

Change Prices, Stock, Photos and New Items in Square Square is the single source of truth. Product names, sizes, prices, photos and stock counts come from your Square catalogue and are not edited here. Sales at the Square till reduce online stock too. Changes made in Square usually appear on the website within minutes.

  • Search by name, French name or SKU.
  • Filter by category (includes its sub-categories), stock state (In Stock, Low Stock, Out of Stock, Untracked in Square), or return class.
  • The header shows when the last Square sync ran.

Back-in-stock emails: out-of-stock pages let customers leave their email. As soon as Square shows the item back in stock, each of them is emailed once with a link to the product. Nothing for staff to do.

Section 7: Kitchen Recipes, Ingredients & Substitutions

Sidebar: Catalog & Content → Kitchen Recipes (/admin/recipes).

7.1 Why Recipes Sell Groceries

Each recipe has an "Add all ingredients to basket" button that adds the exact store products. Recipes also power Cook With What I Have, Cook on a Budget and the "Cook with" section on product pages.

7.2 The Three Tabs

  • Recipes & Stage Gate: every recipe with its approval status, ingredient list and estimated kit cost for 4 people.
  • Ingredients & Shelf Variations: cooking ingredients (e.g. "palm oil") linked to the store products and sizes that supply them. Rank 1 is the preferred one. Ingredients can be marked Pantry Staple (not added to the basket).
  • Curated Substitutions: chef-approved swaps offered when an ingredient is out of stock.

7.3 Publishing a Recipe

  1. Click New Recipe. Fill in the title, cuisine, origin country, servings, prep and cook times, intro, and photo.
  2. Add ingredients from the ingredient list, with quantities. Mark optional ones as optional.
  3. Write numbered cooking steps and save. The recipe is saved as a Draft.
  4. When every check passes, press Approve Live. The recipe then appears on the storefront.
Stage Gate: Why "Approve Live" Is Blocked A recipe can't go live until it has steps, a valid cuisine, at least one required ingredient, and store products linked to every ingredient. Any missing item is listed on the recipe card. Fix it in the Ingredients tab with Link Variations.

Use the star to feature a recipe on the homepage.

Section 8: Stories & Updates

Sidebar: Catalog & Content → Stories & Updates (/admin/stories). The red badge counts reader comments waiting for approval.

8.1 Two Kinds of Post

  • Food Story: cultural and food storytelling, shown at /food-stories.
  • Brand Update: announcements, new arrivals and store news, shown at /brand-updates.

8.2 Writing a Post

  1. Click New Food Story or New Brand Update.
  2. Fill in the title (the web address is suggested for you), optional author, a short summary for the listing cards, and the story text.
  3. Formatting: leave a blank line between paragraphs. Start a line with ## for a heading. Use **bold** and *italics*.
  4. Add a cover photo (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB; wide photos look best) and an estimated read time.
  5. Choose Published or Draft (hidden). Leave the publish date empty to publish now, or pick a future date to schedule it.
  6. Optionally tick Show in Trending Stories and Allow comments, then save.

8.3 Linking Products and Recipes

In the editor, use Products in this story and Recipes in this story: type at least two letters, pick from the list, and remove with ×. The order you pick them in is the order shown.

  • The story page shows Shop this story (product cards with Add to basket) and Cook from this story (recipes).
  • Each linked product and recipe page shows the story under The Story Behind It, once it's published.
  • Only link what is genuinely related. Nothing appears where no link is made.

8.4 Moderating Comments

Open the Comments tab. Approve a comment to show it, Reject (or Hide) to keep it off the site, or Delete it permanently.

Unpublish Instead of Delete Deleting a post also deletes its comments and can't be undone. To take a post down for now, unpublish it.

Section 9: Homepage Banners

Sidebar: Catalog & Content → Homepage Banners (/admin/banners). All staff can use it.

9.1 The Two Slideshows

  • Top of homepage (large): the first thing customers see.
  • Middle of homepage: a second slideshow further down.

Each slideshow lists its slides in the order they rotate, with a status: Live, Scheduled, Ended or Hidden. A slideshow with no live slides is simply left off the homepage.

9.2 Adding or Editing a Banner

  1. Click + New Banner (or + Add slide on a slideshow), or Edit on an existing one.
  2. Photo: JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB. Use a wide photo (about 2400 × 700) with the subject on the right, because the text sits on the left.
  3. Fill in the Headline and, if you like, a Small heading (top slideshow only), Short text and Button text.
  4. Button goes to: the part of the store address after isaleeko.com, e.g. /shop?sort=new, /shop/country/nigeria or /recipes/jollof-rice.
  5. Pick a Background colour. The preview at the top of the form shows how the slide will look.
  6. Optional: add French text for shoppers using Français.
  7. Press Save banner. It shows on the homepage straight away.

9.3 Scheduling, Hiding and Ordering

  • Starts / Ends: set dates (Atlantic Time) so a promotion banner appears and disappears by itself. Leave them empty to show it until you hide it.
  • Hide / Show: take a banner down for now without losing it.
  • ↑ / ↓: change the order slides rotate in.
  • Delete: removes it for good.
Keep Offers Accurate

Banners are free text, so a banner saying "20% off" doesn't create a discount. Set up the matching offer in Promotions, and give time-limited banners an end date.

Section 10: Hours & Closures

Sidebar: Store Controls → Hours & Closures (/admin/hours). All times are Atlantic Time.

10.1 Weekly Opening Hours

For each day, tick whether the store is open, then enter the Open and Close times (e.g. 10:00 and 19:00). Press Save Opening Hours. Pickup slots are created from these hours, using the pickup settings in Store Settings.

10.2 Holiday & One-Off Closures

  1. Under Store Closures & Holidays, choose the Closure Date.
  2. Add a reason (e.g. "Christmas Day", "Stock-take").
  3. Press Add Closure. Customers can no longer book pickup on that date.

To reopen a date, press Remove next to it.

Emergency Closure (Snowstorm / Power Outage) There is no separate emergency switch. To close today at short notice, add a closure for today's date. New pickups for today stop at once and the dashboard shows "Closed for Holiday". Contact customers with orders already booked for today from the Orders Queue, and reschedule them.

Section 12: Promotions

Managers only. Sidebar: Store Controls → Promotions (/admin/promotions).

How Discounts Work Promotions apply automatically. There are no promo or voucher codes. Each order gets one discount: whichever saves the customer more, a promotion or a Square Loyalty reward. Every promotion must have an end date and stops by itself.

12.1 Creating a First Online Order Offer

  1. Click Create promotion and choose First online order offer. Only customers with no previous order qualify, checked by account and email.
  2. Enter the Name customers see in their order summary, and the objective.
  3. Set the discount: Percent off or Dollars off.
  4. Set Starts and Ends (required), plus an optional Minimum basket, Limit per customer and Budget cap (the most orders it can be used on).
  5. Press Preview to check who is eligible and see an example price. Then press Activate, or Save as draft.

12.2 Managing Promotions

  • Pause / Resume: stop the offer for now, then restart it.
  • End now: ends the offer for good. It can't be restarted.
  • Edit / Delete: available for drafts.
  • Each promotion shows how many times it has been used.

Section 13: Loyalty (Square Loyalty)

Managers only. Sidebar: Store Controls → Loyalty (/admin/loyalty).

13.1 How It Works

  • The website uses your Square Loyalty programme, the same one as the till.
  • Customers join with the phone number they give at checkout. Paid online orders earn points.
  • Signed-in customers can spend a reward at checkout. Points are held while the order is unpaid and given back if it isn't paid.
  • Earning rules and rewards are set in the Square Dashboard → Loyalty, not on the website.

13.2 The Loyalty Screen

AreaWhat It Shows

Last 30 daysOrders that earned points · Points given online · Customers earning online · Rewards used online · Waiting to reach Square
Look up a customer's pointsEnter a phone number to see their balance, lifetime points and member-since date.
Website orders in the programmeOrder, customer, phone, points earned, and reward status (used, given back, or held until paid).

If Square was briefly unreachable, points wait and are retried automatically. Press Retry waiting orders to send them now. In-store activity and full reports stay in the Square Dashboard.

Section 15: Payment Methods & Delivery Switch

Managers only. Sidebar: Store Controls → Payment Methods (/admin/payments).

15.1 Payment Methods

MethodHow It Works
Pay by CardVisa, Mastercard, Amex and debit cards through Square's secure card form.
Apple Pay / Google PayOne-tap wallet payment on supported phones and browsers, through Square.
Interac e-TransferThe customer sends money to the store's e-Transfer email. Staff confirm it in the Orders Queue before packing. Customers are asked to pay within 24 hours. Cancel unpaid ones by hand with Cancel (Not Paid).

Square Keys Are Not Shown in the Portal For security, the Square access token, location ID and application ID are stored only in the hosting environment (Render). They are not visible or editable in the portal. Never send these keys by email or text message. Ask your developer to change them, for example when switching to a new Square account.

Section 16: Square Check & Catalogue Pull

Managers only. Sidebar: Store Controls → Square Check (/admin/square-check).

16.1 Check Square (Read-Only)

Press the check button to compare Square's categories, sub-categories, items and prices with the website. Nothing is changed on either side. You'll see counts for both, then either Square and the website are in step or a list of differences to look at.

16.2 Pull Everything from Square

Pull from Square now copies every item, category, price, photo and stock count from Square onto the website, and hides demo products that aren't in Square. It runs in the background and the page updates by itself, showing when the last pull finished or failed.

  • When to use it: after switching Square accounts (e.g. from test to live), after big catalogue changes, or if the check shows differences.
  • It changes the website only, never Square.
Normal Day-to-Day Sync Square sends changes to the website automatically, so you don't need to pull after every edit. The page header shows whether you are connected to live or sandbox (test) Square.

17.2 Resetting a Forgotten Password

  1. Find the employee in the staff list (search by name, email or phone).
  2. Click Edit & Password.
  3. In the Reset Password box, press Generate Random or type a new password.
  4. Save, and give the new password to the employee.

17.3 Departing Staff

  • Deactivate (Toggle): the employee is locked out at once. Their past order history stays.
  • Revoke access (Trash icon): if the employee has handled orders, their admin rights are removed and the history is kept. Otherwise the account is deleted.
  • Self-Protection Guard: you can't deactivate or delete your own account, so you can't lock yourself out by accident.

18.4 Emergency Access Recovery (For IT & Developers)

If every manager is locked out, a developer can restore access from the server terminal:

Run on the host server (Render → web service → Shell, available on paid Render plans):

php artisan staff:create owner@isaleeko.com --name="Store Owner" --password="<new strong password>" --role=manager

This creates or reactivates the account as an active Store Manager. Sign in, then change the password straight away.
